# The holonomy embedding

*A machine-learning embedding constructed and optimized by ants with
high-dimensional integrative capacities through decidable portals.*

This is the design document for the extension built on the path-
topological network of `ant_extras` (see the crate README for the
network itself). It states the proposal, what was built of it, what was
measured, and where it stops.

## The proposal

The object embedded is not a point but a **route**. Everything the
crate does well is about routes: the word of a route is its homotopy
class, the symbol registry stores classes, and a thought's home vector
is the integral of its displacement. The embedding generalises that
integral from two dimensions to D and makes it the representation.

Every thought carries an integrated state x in R^D, its
*high-dimensional integrative capacity*, starting where its trip sets
out. On a free walk a step adds the step itself, turned back by the
turns the body's frame has made through folds, so that the walk
*develops* in the frame it set out in. On a walk of states a move adds
the move's **transport**, an affine map of the state, translations in
what was built. A move is also a **letter**: a portal crossed, a
relation followed. A route's word is its class, and when the transports
are consistent the state a route arrives with, less the state it set
out with, depends only on the word. That is the classical fact that on
a flat connection the development of a path depends only on its
homotopy class, and a loop's development is its holonomy.

So the embedding is a representation of path classes in the affine
group: each registered symbol carries a transport, the mean of what the
routes of its class integrate, and the spread of those integrals about
the mean is the curvature signal, zero on a flat connection. Words are
the discrete part of the representation and transports the continuous
part. Composition is exact: the transport of a concatenated word is the
composition of the transports, so unseen compositions of known classes
have vectors before any thought walks them.

**Decidable portals** are what let one medium carry many relations. A
portal, or a move, admits a thought only if a predicate on its state
and its word so far holds: in the relational build, not the query
relation from the head, not straight back, not beyond a few hops.
Admission is decided per crossing in bounded time, so a walk is a typed
computation, the same charts support different admissible routes for
different states, and generation by search over places, words and gate
states terminates.

There is no gradient. The two signals are the ones ants use:

* **Closure.** A trip should integrate back to where its target is.
  The residual is credited back to the transports of the moves crossed,
  a share each, and to the target's own transport, which is the ants'
  recalibration at the nest turned into learning. Where a class of
  loops keeps a residual, that residual is its invariant.
* **Arrival.** Food is an arrival whose integrated state lies near a
  target vector, the quality falling off with the distance. Trail is
  laid home in proportion to quality along the moves crossed, so which
  moves to take is learned by the trail as now, and the symbols' yields
  and precisions rank the classes.

Inference and generation come from the existing machinery: the word of
a route is its class, its integrated state its vector, a name can be
attached after the fact; a class is generated by search or expressed
for the colony to walk.

## What was built

The core generalisation (`topos.rs`, `problem.rs`, `thought.rs`,
`mind.rs`):

* A route carries the letters of its moves beside its places. Letters
  come in three ranges: ray crossings (from the punctures), **moves**
  over the surface (a portal crossed; a move followed by its reverse
  cancels, as a crossing does), and **links** (a relation followed; a
  link followed and then followed backwards leads somewhere else, a
  parent's other child, so links never cancel). A trip's word may begin
  with a **prefix** (a query relation), so that classes are classes of
  that query's routes.
* A problem may declare an integrative capacity D, the state a trip
  sets out with, the transport of a move, a target vector, a quality
  and a scent that depend on the integrated state, the letter of a
  move, a different origin per trip, and a learning hook called with
  every trip brought home or given up.
* A thought carries x and the letters of its moves; a free walk
  develops its steps into x through the turns of the folds it crossed
  (a portal crossed is a letter of the move, and `Warp` now says which
  portal and which way); a walk of states integrates each move.
* A symbol carries a transport (the mean of what its routes integrate),
  a spread, and a count of misses beside its support, so a rule has a
  precision.
* Generation runs over any neighbourhood: cells through portals on the
  surface (`Mind::generate`), or states of a problem
  (`Mind::generate_states`), a breadth-first search over places and the
  words of the ways to them.

Two instantiations:

* **Windings on a cylinder** (`tests/thinking.rs`): a region whose north
  edge is joined to its south edge, a start four cells above a goal. The
  direct class crosses nothing and develops four cells south; the class
  through the fold carries the portal's letter and develops a
  circumference less, its transport twelve cells north with a spread
  under four. Generation through the fold produces the way once round
  and twice round. The colony computes the development of its routes,
  and the registry their holonomy: the universal cover of the ring,
  read off the walks.
* **Relations** (`problems/relations.rs`): a knowledge graph of
  families. Entities have places on a chart in family blobs; the
  relations are `parent`, `sibling` and `spouse` as base facts and
  `grandparent`, `uncle` and `cousin` as derived facts, a share of which
  is held out. A query `(head, relation, ?)` is a trip: it sets out from
  the head with the query relation as the first letter of its word and
  its state at the head's vector; a move follows a relation and adds
  its vector; the gates forbid the query relation from the head, going
  straight back, and more than three hops; food is a known answer, its
  quality sharpened by how near the state has come to the head's vector
  plus the query's; the scent of a move is how near it would bring the
  state. The word of a trip brought home is a rule, `grandparent:
  parent parent`. Learning is the closure credit on the path's relation
  vectors plus a translational update of the entity vectors with a
  random negative, both local, both at delivery. Held-out facts are
  ranked by the geometry, by the rules the network holds (each rule
  followed from the head, weighted by its precision and support), and
  by both.

## What was measured

**Windings on a cylinder** (`cargo test --release -p ant_extras
windings`): a ring 14 cells wide and 16 tall between its joined edges,
32 thoughts, 3 000 ticks. The direct class (word `1`) develops four
cells south, the class through the fold (word `m0` or `m0'`) develops
twelve cells north, both within a cell and a half of the exact
transport, with a spread under four. Generation through the fold gives
the way once round (at least twelve moves) and twice round (twelve
more), and the direct way in four.

**Relations** (`cargo run --release -p ant_extras --example
relations`, 30 families): 356 entities, 1 653 facts, 585 queries, 293
held-out facts; 64 thoughts, 12 000 ticks, learning rate 0.01, in
about 90 s.

| | MRR | hits@1 | hits@10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| before, random vectors | 0.011 | 0.000 | 0.010 |
| after, geometry alone | 0.280 | 0.072 | 0.795 |
| after, rules alone | 0.911 | 0.829 | 1.000 |
| after, both | 0.895 | 0.799 | 1.000 |

215 rules registered, every one an exact word with zero spread. The
most walked: `cousin: parent uncle'` (3 370 trips home), `cousin:
grandparent grandparent'`, `cousin: uncle parent'`, `sibling: parent
parent'`, `sibling: cousin cousin`, `grandparent: cousin' grandparent`,
`grandparent: uncle parent`. The planted rules close: the distance
between a body's summed relation vectors and its query's falls from
1.00, 2.51, 1.37 and 1.26 (sibling, grandparent, uncle, cousin) to 0.01,
0.01, 0.01 and 0.00, and the mean quality of arrivals, half of which is
the geometric match, reaches 0.999. A route of the cousin rule
generated from a held-out cousin's head lands on the held-out cousin.

The geometry alone predicts poorly because it closes so well: a
translational embedding can only make a symmetric relation close by
sending it to zero, so `sibling`, and with it `cousin` (`parent sibling
parent'`), collapse onto the head, and their answers are told apart
from the head's other relatives by nothing but the rules. Before the
development leak in the first build was fixed, the leak's noise kept
the geometry from collapsing and it scored 0.60 alone; the exact
transports are worth more than that accident.

## Where it stops

* The translational transport is a coarse invariant. `cousin` is
  `parent sibling parent'`, whose translation is `sibling`'s, and a
  symmetric relation closes only at zero, so the geometry cannot tell a
  cousin from a sibling from the head; the words can, which is why the
  rules predict better than the geometry. Rotations per relation (the
  affine transport the proposal allows) would lift this and were left
  for later.
* Every trip brought home credits its residual to its relations, so
  rules that contradict each other in the geometry pull the vectors
  about; the learning rate has to be small against the rate of
  deliveries, and the transports' spread is the honest measure of how
  consistent the geometry has become.
* The trail on the chart between entity places carries little for a
  graph, whose structure is in its moves: the scent (the geometry) and
  the symbol channels (the rules) do the routing. The chart earns its
  place by giving the trips a medium, a history and a place to lay
  marks, and would earn more with entity places laid out by the learned
  vectors themselves.
* A word is bounded (five letters with the query), so a rule has at
  most four hops, and a route's class is its class from its own origin.
